Recommended Approach

Two phases.
Clear priorities.

Phase 1 creates commercial control by 30 June 2026. Phase 2 extends HubSpot into a broader event revenue and delivery architecture.

Phase 1 — By 30 June 2026

Commercial Control Foundation

  • Sales pipeline governance — rationalise structure, define stage criteria, required fields and loss reasons
  • Mandatory data capture — amount, close date, owner, contact + company association
  • Routing and inactivity management — lead assignment, inactive deal rules
  • Active pipeline data clean-up — focus on commercially active records first
  • Core reporting — pipeline, activity, data quality, lead source dashboards
  • Marketing Hub governance — campaign naming, UTMs, list hygiene, form audit
  • Workflow dependency review — subscription, suppression, lifecycle, routing
  • Clay enrichment pilot — controlled writeback with confidence rules
  • Limited AI use cases — summaries, qualification support, follow-up drafts
  • Contracts + payments discovery — map current process, MVP only
  • Integration governance — review Xero model before further automation
  • Training and adoption programme
Phase 2 — Beyond June 2026

Event-Commerce Architecture

  • Full ExpoFP integration — availability, reservations, bookings, payment failure handling
  • Advanced event architecture — event editions, stand or booking custom objects
  • Full Xero and finance automation — invoice ownership, reconciliation, error handling
  • Deeper campaign, event and revenue attribution framework
  • Advanced AI agents and customer-facing AI journeys
  • Service Hub expansion and customer service process design
  • Broader workflow rationalisation and legacy automation retirement
  • Historic data remediation beyond active commercial records
  • Integration estate simplification and private app review
Phase 1 Success Measure

Mash should be able to trust HubSpot to show who owns each active opportunity, what it is worth, when it is expected to close, who is associated, what the next action is, whether it is inactive, where it came from and what contract/payment status applies.

Target Architecture

Seven principles
to build toward.

These principles should guide every configuration decision in Phase 1 and Phase 2.

HubSpot as Commercial System of Record

HubSpot governs contacts, companies, deals, activities, lifecycle, pipeline, campaign source and commercial reporting. It does not need to own every finance or delivery record.

Xero as Finance System of Record

Given existing Xero connections and invoice history, Xero should likely remain the finance source of truth, with HubSpot holding commercial and visibility data — subject to discovery validation.

Controlled Clay Enrichment

Clay should enrich selected segments through a pilot, with strict writeback rules, source tracking, confidence scoring and no uncontrolled overwrites of trusted HubSpot fields.

AI Inside Governed Workflows

AI should assist users and managers within clearly defined handoff rules — supporting qualification, summarisation, routing and follow-up drafts. Autonomous AI actions come later once data quality is stable.

Event Model Before ExpoFP

Mash must define event, edition, exhibitor, sponsor and product relationships inside HubSpot before building any floor plan integration. Architecture first, integration second.

Reporting from Governed Process

Dashboards should be built only after required fields, associations and stage rules are defined. Building reports before governance simply exposes data quality problems on a screen.

Integration Governance Before Expansion

Existing connected apps, Xero integrations and private apps must be reviewed and rationalised before adding new automation or middleware. Adding complexity before resolving existing issues creates compounding risk.
